= Creamy Chicken and Corn Chowder
serves: 7 (about 14 cups)
time: Prep 15 min · Cook 40 min
from: Cooking Classy | cookingclassy.com
[Ingredients]
- 3 cups cooked shredded chicken (rotisserie works well)
- 8 slices bacon, cooked and chopped
- ¼ cup butter
- 1 large red bell pepper, diced (1½ cups)
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced (1¼ cups)
- 1–2 jalapeños, finely chopped (seeded for less heat)
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
- 6 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 3 medium russet potatoes (22 oz), peeled and cut just under ½ inch (3 cups)
- 2 bay leaves
- Salt and black pepper
- 2½ cups fresh or frozen corn
- 1½ cups half-and-half
- Green onions and shredded cheese, to serve (optional)
[Method]
- Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add the bell pepper, onion and jalapeño and cook about 3 minutes, until tender.
- Add the garlic for 30 seconds, then stir in the flour and cook 2 minutes, stirring constantly.
- Gradually stir in the broth. Add the potatoes and bay leaves and season with salt and pepper.
- Bring to a simmer, stirring often, then turn the heat to low and simmer uncovered 20–25 minutes, until the potatoes are tender.
- Stir in the chicken, corn and half-and-half and simmer 5 minutes more. Fish out the bay leaves.
- Serve topped with the bacon, plus green onions and cheese if you like.
[Tips]
- Starting with raw chicken? Add about 1 lb of breasts or thighs with the potatoes, cook until 165°F (74°C), then shred and return to the pot.

= Egg Drop Soup
serves: 6
time: 15 min
from: The Woks of Life | thewoksoflife.com
[Ingredients]
- 4 cups (1 liter) chicken stock
- ½ tsp sesame oil
- ¾ tsp salt
- ⅛ tsp sugar
- ⅛ tsp white pepper
- ¼ tsp MSG (optional)
- ½ tsp ground turmeric (optional, for color)
- 3 tbsp cornstarch mixed with ⅓ cup water
- 3 eggs, lightly beaten
- 1 scallion, chopped
[Method]
- Bring the stock to a simmer in a medium pot. Stir in the sesame oil, salt, sugar, white pepper and MSG, if using.
- Add the turmeric for color, if using. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
- Re-stir the cornstarch slurry (it settles fast), then drizzle it in slowly while stirring constantly. Add more or less for a thicker or thinner soup, simmering a minute or two between additions.
- Stir the soup in a circle with a ladle and slowly pour in the beaten eggs in a thin stream. Stir faster for fine ribbons, slower for big ones.
- Ladle into bowls and top with the scallion.

= Instant Pot Fish Stew
serves: 4
time: Prep 5 min · Cook 15 min, plus 10 min to come to pressure
from: Bowl of Delicious | bowlofdelicious.com
[Ingredients]
- 4 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 1 medium red onion, quartered and thinly sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, roughly chopped
- ½ cup dry white wine (such as pinot grigio or sauvignon blanc)
- 8 oz bottled clam juice
- 2½ cups water
- ½ lb red or gold potatoes, diced
- 1 can (15 oz) diced tomatoes, or 1½ cups diced fresh tomatoes with their juice
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- Pinch of crushed red pepper
- 2 lb sea bass, cut into roughly 2-inch pieces
- 2 tbsp lemon juice (about 1 lemon)
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh dill
- Crusty bread, to serve
[Method]
- On the Sauté setting, cook the onion in 2 tbsp of the oil for about 3 minutes, until softened and browned.
- Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds, until fragrant.
- Pour in the wine and scrape up the browned bits. Let it bubble about 1 minute, until reduced by half.
- Add the clam juice, water, potatoes, tomatoes, plenty of salt and pepper and the crushed red pepper.
- Turn off Sauté, seal the lid and cook at High pressure for 5 minutes. Quick-release the pressure.
- Switch back to Sauté. When the stew simmers, add the fish and simmer about 5 minutes, until it just flakes apart.
- Turn off the heat and stir in the lemon juice, dill and the remaining 2 tbsp oil. Season to taste and serve with crusty bread.
[Tips]
- Any firm white fish works: cod, monkfish or halibut.
- **Stovetop:** Start the same way in a large pot, then simmer covered about 20 minutes, until the potatoes are tender. Add the fish for the last 5 minutes.
- **Slow cooker:** Add everything except the fish, lemon juice, dill and final 2 tbsp oil. Cook on Low 6 hours or High 3 hours, add the fish for the last 20 minutes, then stir in the finishing ingredients.
- No clam juice (or a shellfish allergy)? Use fish stock, or one more cup of water.
- Written for a 6-quart pot; halve it for a 3-quart mini.

= Mallard Creek Brunswick Stew
makes: about 16 pints
time: Two days (meat is cooked and chilled overnight)
from: Cooks.com | cooks.com
[Ingredients]
- 4 lb beef chuck
- 1 lb Boston butt (pork shoulder)
- 1 stewing hen, about 5 lb
- ¼ lb (1 stick) butter
- Salt and pepper
- 3 quarts canned crushed tomatoes
- ¾ lb long-grain rice, uncooked
- 1 can (15 oz) creamed corn
[Method]
- **Day 1:** Simmer the beef, pork and hen in water (together or separately) until tender. Save the broth. Let the meat cool, then remove the bones and the chicken skin.
- Grind all the meat in a food processor or meat grinder. Refrigerate the meat and the broth overnight.
- **Day 2:** Strain the broth into a large pot. Add the rice, butter, salt and pepper and cook about 30 minutes, stirring constantly, until the rice is tender.
- Add the tomatoes and corn. Once it's back to a simmer, cook about 20 minutes.
- Stir in the ground meat and cook 10 minutes more. Stir often, adding hot water if the stew starts to stick.
[Tips]
- The original also gives a larger batch, cooked the same way: 6 lb beef chuck, a 5-lb hen, 1 lb rice, 2 cans creamed corn, 2 cans lima beans, 3 large cans stewed tomatoes, 6 chopped onions, ¼ cup salt, 1 tbsp pepper and up to 1 lb butter (optional).
- This makes a big batch, and it freezes well in pint containers.

= Pressure Cooker Roast Chicken Stock
makes: a little over 2 quarts
time: About 1 hr
from: Chris Young on YouTube | youtube.com
[Ingredients]
- 1 store-bought rotisserie chicken
- ½ onion, thinly sliced
- ½ carrot, peeled and thinly sliced
- 2 garlic cloves, smashed
- 2 quarts water
- 1–2 tbsp chicken fat or neutral oil
[Method]
- Bring the water to a boil in a kettle or separate pot.
- Heat the fat in the pressure cooker on Sauté and sweat the carrot, onion and garlic until soft and lightly colored.
- Meanwhile, strip all the meat off the chicken and shred it finely. Add the shredded meat, skin and carcass to the pot. (More surface area means faster, fuller extraction.)
- Pour in the boiling water, seal, and bring to High pressure. Cook 40 minutes; 30–90 minutes is the sweet spot for chicken.
- Don't quick-release. Let the pressure drop naturally (or release it very slowly) so the stock doesn't churn and turn cloudy.
- Strain through a fine sieve. Chill, then lift off the fat.
[Tips]
- For beef stock, go about 2 hours. Past roughly 3 hours under pressure, stock turns metallic and bitter and won't set to a gel.
- **Crystal-clear consommé, the easy way:** freeze the stock in cubes, then let them thaw slowly in a lined strainer set over a bowl in the fridge. The liquid that drips through is clear; the fat and haze stay behind.
- **Raft method:** blend some of the stock with a little raw chicken until frothy, stir it back into the pot, bring to High pressure, then let it cool naturally. A raft forms on top and traps the cloudiness; strain through a sieve.

= Savory Pork Stew
serves: 8
time: Prep 15 min · Cook 2 hr 10 min
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Mitzy Renee | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 lb cubed pork stew meat
- Garlic powder, salt and pepper
- 2 tbsp cornstarch, or as needed
- 8 red potatoes, cut into chunks
- 1 green bell pepper, chopped
- 1 red bell pepper, chopped
- 1 sweet onion, diced
- 1 can (11 oz) whole kernel corn
- 1 can (14 oz) stewed tomatoes
- 1 can (14 oz) beef broth
- 1 can (10.75 oz) condensed cream of mushroom soup
- 1¼ cups milk
- 1 tbsp Italian seasoning
[Method]
- Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Season the pork all over with garlic powder, salt and pepper, then dust it lightly with cornstarch. Brown it lightly in the hot oil; it doesn't need to cook through.
- Move the pork to a slow cooker and add the potatoes, bell peppers, onion and corn.
- Mix the tomatoes, broth, mushroom soup, milk and Italian seasoning in a bowl and pour it into the slow cooker.
- Cook on High for 1 hour, then on Low for at least 1 hour more.
[Tips]
- Serve with crusty bread for dipping.

= The Best Chili
serves: 8 (about 1½ cups each)
time: Prep 20 min · Cook 45–60 min
from: Spend With Pennies | spendwithpennies.com
[Ingredients]
- 2 lb lean ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and finely diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2½ tbsp chili powder, divided
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 green bell pepper, diced
- 1 can (14.5 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 can (19 oz) red kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, with juice
- 1½ cups beef broth
- 1 cup beer
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp brown sugar (optional)
- Salt and pepper
- Shredded cheddar, green onions and cilantro, to serve
[Method]
- Mix the beef with 1½ tbsp of the chili powder.
- Brown the beef with the onion, jalapeño and garlic in a large pot, then drain off the fat.
- Add the remaining 1 tbsp chili powder, the cumin, bell pepper, both cans of tomatoes, beans, broth, beer, tomato paste and sugar. Bring to a boil.
- Turn down the heat and simmer uncovered 45–60 minutes, until it's as thick as you like. Season with salt and pepper and serve with your favorite toppings.
[Tips]
- Swap the beer for more beef broth if you prefer.
- Any ground meat works here.
