= Baked Chicken Katsu
serves: 4
time: Prep 20 min · Cook 40 min
temp: 400°F / 200°C (375°F convection)
from: Just One Cookbook | justonecookbook.com
[Ingredients: Toasted panko]
- 1¼ cups panko
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
[Ingredients: Chicken]
- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 1 large egg
- ½ tbsp neutral oil
- ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- Tonkatsu sauce, to serve
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C) with a rack in the middle (25°F lower for convection).
- Toast the panko with the oil in a frying pan over medium heat, stirring often, until golden. Tip into a shallow dish to cool.
- Butterfly each breast: cut a slit down the center lengthwise without cutting all the way through, open the sides out like a book, then cut in half to make two cutlets. Pound them to an even ¼–½ inch thickness.
- Season both sides with salt (use half as much if it's table salt) and pepper.
- Set out three shallow dishes: the egg beaten with the ½ tbsp oil, the flour, and the toasted panko.
- Coat each cutlet in flour and shake off the excess, dip it in egg, then press it firmly into the panko.
- Arrange on a wire rack set over a rimmed baking sheet and bake 25–30 minutes, until 165°F (74°C) inside.
- Slice into ¾-inch strips and serve with tonkatsu sauce.
[Tips]
- Toasting the panko first gives the deep golden color of fried katsu without the frying.
- Leftovers keep 3 days in the fridge or 1 month frozen. Reheat at 350°F for 15–20 minutes (about 30 minutes from frozen).

= California Rolls
makes: 6 rolls (48 pieces)
time: 20 min, plus making the sushi rice
from: No Recipes (Marc Matsumoto) | norecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 1 batch sushi rice (see Perfect Sushi Rice in this chapter)
- 1 medium avocado, cut into 16–20 thin wedges
- 1 small cucumber, seeded and cut into thin strips (about ⅕ inch)
- 200 g (7 oz) crab meat or imitation crab
- 6 half-sheets nori (about 4 × 7½ in)
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar stirred into 1 cup water, for your hands
- Soy sauce, wasabi and pickled ginger, to serve
[Method]
- Stir the vinegar into the water to make a hand-dip. If using a bamboo mat, wrap it in plastic wrap.
- Lay a half-sheet of nori on the mat near the edge closest to you. With damp fingers, spread a generous handful of rice evenly over the nori, leaving a ¼-inch strip bare along the far edge. Spread gently so you don't crush the grains.
- Sprinkle the rice with sesame seeds, then flip the sheet over so the rice faces down and the bare strip is nearest you.
- Lay avocado wedges across the middle of the nori, overlapping the thin ends, then add a cucumber strip and the crab. Don't overfill (split imitation crab sticks lengthwise if needed).
- Tuck your thumbs under the mat, hold the filling with your fingers and roll away from you until the edge of the rice meets the bare nori. Press lightly to seal and keep rolling until the seam is underneath.
- Squeeze the roll gently through the mat, top and sides, to firm it up evenly.
- With a sharp, wet knife and a back-and-forth sawing motion, cut the roll in half. Line up the halves and cut them into 4 pieces each.
- Serve with soy sauce, wasabi and ginger.

= Creamy Rose Tteokbokki
from: FutureDish | futuredish.com
[Ingredients]
- 2 cups tteokbokki rice cakes (thawed if frozen)
- 5–6 broccoli florets, halved
- ⅓ onion, thinly sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- A few sausages, sliced into rounds (or 2 strips bacon)
- 1 sheet fish cake, cut into bite-size pieces (optional)
- 2 eggs
- Cooking oil
[Ingredients: Creamy sauce]
- ¾ cup heavy cream
- ¾ cup milk
- 1½ tbsp gochujang
- ½ tbsp gochugaru (Korean chili flakes)
- 2 big pinches of salt
- A few grinds of black pepper
- 1 slice sandwich cheese (or a handful of shredded cheese)
- Grated Parmesan, to finish
[Method]
- If the rice cakes are frozen, soak them in water to thaw while you prep everything else.
- Bring a pot of water to a boil. Blanch the broccoli for 30 seconds and scoop it out. Lower the eggs into the same water and boil 7 minutes, then cool in cold water and peel.
- Drain the rice cakes. Heat a little oil in a frying pan over medium heat and stir-fry the garlic, onion and sausage until the onion is translucent.
- Pour in the cream and milk and stir in the gochujang and gochugaru. Add the rice cakes and let it bubble a few minutes, until they soften.
- Add the broccoli, the eggs and the fish cake, and season with salt and pepper. Stir in the cheese and simmer until the sauce is thick and creamy.
- Serve topped with a little grated Parmesan.

= Dim Sum Chicken Feet (Pressure Cooker)
serves: 4
time: Prep 10 min · Cook 45 min, plus 1–2 hr soaking
from: What To Cook Today | whattocooktoday.com
[Ingredients]
- 500 g (about 1 lb) chicken feet
- 1 tbsp salt, for scrubbing
- Oil, for frying (or for coating if air-frying or baking)
- Ice water
[Ingredients: Braise]
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
- 1 star anise
- ¼ cup Shaoxing wine
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- ¼ cup rock sugar (or 1 tbsp granulated or brown sugar)
- 2 red chilies, finely chopped
- Water, just enough to cover
[Ingredients: To finish]
- 2 tsp cornstarch mixed with 1 tbsp water (optional)
- 1 green onion (green part), finely chopped
[Method]
- Clip the nails off the feet and cut away any discolored spots. Rub all over with the salt, rinse, and pat completely dry.
- Fry until golden, by one of these methods:
  - **Deep-fry:** in batches in hot oil for about 5 minutes, until pale golden. Use a lid; they spatter.
  - **Air fryer:** coat in oil and air-fry at 400°F (200°C) for 15 minutes, shake, then 15 minutes more.
  - **Oven:** coat in oil and bake at 375°F (190°C) for 30 minutes, turning halfway.
- If deep-fried, drop them straight into ice water and soak 1–2 hours, until puffed. This gives the wrinkly dim-sum texture.
- On Sauté, heat 1 tbsp oil in the pressure cooker. Cook the garlic and ginger 1 minute, add the star anise for 30 seconds, then deglaze with the Shaoxing wine, scraping the bottom.
- Add the feet, oyster sauce, both soy sauces, sugar and chilies and stir. Pour in water just to cover.
- Pressure cook on High for 35 minutes (add 3–5 minutes if they're not tender enough), then let the pressure release naturally.
- Switch to Sauté, bring to a boil and stir in the cornstarch slurry to lightly thicken the sauce. Scatter with green onion and serve.
[Tips]
- **Stovetop:** Follow the same steps in a covered pot and simmer gently for 1½–2 hours, until tender.

= Kabocha Squash Stir-Fry (Pad Faktong)
serves: 4
time: Prep 15 min · Cook 10 min
from: Hot Thai Kitchen (Pailin Chongchitnant) | hot-thai-kitchen.com
[Ingredients]
- 500 g (about 1 lb) kabocha squash, cut into 1-inch wedges, then into ¼-inch slices
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 5 garlic cloves, roughly chopped
- 3 eggs
- 1 heaping cup Thai basil leaves
- Jasmine rice, to serve
- Prik nam pla (chili fish sauce), to serve (optional)
[Ingredients: Sauce]
- 1½ tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1½ tsp fish sauce
- 1½ tsp sugar
- ¼ tsp ground white (or black) pepper
- ¼ cup water
[Ingredients: Vegetarian sauce (instead)]
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp Golden Mountain sauce (or Maggi Seasoning)
- 1½ tsp sugar
- ¼ tsp ground white (or black) pepper
- ¼ cup water
[Method]
- Stir the sauce ingredients together until the sugar dissolves.
- Heat the oil in a wok and cook the garlic until it just starts to turn golden.
- Add the squash and toss to coat in the oil, then add the sauce and toss again. Cover and cook over medium heat about 4 minutes, stirring once, until a fork goes through a piece with slight resistance. Add a splash of water if it dries out.
- Push the squash aside to clear a space in the middle. Crack in the eggs and break up the yolks. Lay the squash back over the eggs and let them set without stirring; when they're about 75% cooked, toss gently once or twice so the egg stays in chunks.
- Once the eggs are cooked, turn off the heat and stir in the basil until just wilted. Serve with rice and a drizzle of prik nam pla.
[Tips]
- Don't slice the squash thinner than ¼ inch, or it overcooks and falls apart.

= Perfect Sushi Rice
makes: about 5 cups
time: Prep 5 min · Cook 20 min, plus cooling
from: Allrecipes · recipe by LucyDelRey | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 2 cups uncooked sushi rice
- 3 cups water
- ¼–½ cup rice vinegar (see tips)
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 tsp salt
[Method]
- Rinse the rice in a strainer under cold water until the water runs clear.
- Bring the rice and water to a boil in a saucepan over medium-high heat. Turn the heat to low, cover and cook 12 minutes. Take it off the heat and leave it covered 10 minutes more to finish steaming. Uncover and let cool.
- Meanwhile, warm the vinegar, sugar, oil and salt in a small saucepan over medium heat until the sugar dissolves. Let cool.
- Stir the vinegar mixture into the rice. It looks wet at first but dries as it cools.
[Tips]
- Allrecipes' test kitchen cut the vinegar to ¼ cup; the original recipe uses ½ cup.
- Use it for the California Rolls in this chapter.
