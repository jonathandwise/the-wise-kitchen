= Baked Whole Red Snapper with Garlic
serves: 4
time: Bake about 45 min
temp: 350°F / 175°C
from: Epicurious · from the Admiral's Inn, Antigua | epicurious.com
note: An easy but impressive whole fish from the Admiral's Inn in Antigua, baked in a sealed foil packet with garlic, lemon and thyme. Have the fish market clean and scale the snapper for you.
[Ingredients]
- 1 whole red snapper (2–2¼ lb), cleaned
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- ¾ tsp seafood seasoning
- 2 tbsp (¼ stick) chilled butter, cut into pieces, plus more for the foil
- 6 fresh thyme sprigs
- 6 thin lemon slices
- Salt and pepper
[Method]
- Lay a 24 × 18-inch sheet of foil on a large, heavy baking sheet and butter it. Set the fish in the center.
- Season the cavity with the garlic, lemon juice and seafood seasoning, dot it with the butter and tuck in the thyme sprigs.
- Sprinkle the outside of the fish with salt and pepper and lay the lemon slices on top. Fold the edges of the foil over the fish and seal. (You can get this far a day ahead; keep it refrigerated.)
- Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Bake until the fish is cooked through, about 45 minutes.
- Transfer the fish to a platter and open the foil.
[Tips]
- Can't find a whole snapper? Use fillets and bake them about 25 minutes.

= Cantonese Steamed Fish
from: Souped Up Recipes (Mandy) on YouTube | youtube.com
[Ingredients: Fish and marinade]
- 1 whole white fish, 1.2–1.8 lb (544–820 g) before gutting (snapper, perch, sea bass), scaled and gutted
- 5 garlic cloves (15 g), crushed
- 2-inch piece of ginger (30 g), crushed
- 3 scallions (45 g), crushed
- 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine (optional)
[Ingredients: Seasoned soy sauce]
- ½ dried shiitake mushroom, snipped into pieces
- 3 tbsp hot water
- 2 tsp oil
- 1 tsp whole Sichuan peppercorns
- 1 tsp diced ginger
- 1 tsp diced garlic
- 2 tsp diced scallion
- 3½ tbsp soy sauce
- ½ tbsp fish sauce
- ½ tbsp oyster sauce
- 1½ tsp sugar
[Ingredients: To steam and finish]
- 3-inch piece of ginger (45 g), cut into thick strips
- 4 scallions (60 g), cut into 3-inch lengths, whites and greens separated
- 3 thin slices of ginger
- 1 red chili
- 2½ tbsp finely minced garlic
- 3 tbsp neutral oil
[Method]
- Rinse the cleaned fish well. Cut along the backbone on both sides (and score the thick fillets once or twice if you like) so it cooks evenly.
- Roughly chop the crushed garlic, ginger and scallions and rub them over the fish with the wine. Leave at room temperature while you make the sauce.
- **Sauce:** Soak the shiitake in the hot water for 15 minutes. Warm the oil and Sichuan peppercorns in a small saucepan over low heat for 1 minute. Add the diced ginger, garlic and scallion and stir over medium-low for 3 minutes. Add the soy sauce, fish sauce, oyster sauce, sugar and the mushroom with its soaking water. Cover and simmer on the lowest heat for 3 minutes. Strain if you want it smooth.
- Lay the ginger strips and scallion whites on a heatproof plate to make a rack. Finely julienne the scallion greens, ginger slices and chili, soak them in cold water for at least 10 minutes so they curl, then pat dry.
- Fill your largest steamer with plenty of water and bring it to a full boil over the highest heat. Discard the marinade aromatics, pat the fish dry and set it on the ginger-scallion rack.
- Steam over the highest heat: 6–8 minutes for a 1.2-lb fish, 7–9 for 1.4 lb, 8–10 for 1.6 lb and 10–12 for 1.8 lb. Turn off the heat and leave the lid on 3–5 minutes more. A chopstick should slide into the thickest part with no resistance.
- Move the fish to a clean platter, leaving the steaming liquid behind. Top with the minced garlic and the julienned aromatics. Heat the 3 tbsp oil until smoking and pour it over the aromatics, then spoon the seasoned soy sauce around the fish. Serve with rice.
[Tips]
- Freshness is everything: look for clear, shiny eyes, red gills, glossy skin and a clean smell.
- Steam it in one go. Opening the steamer to check partway through hurts the texture.
- Bottled "seasoned soy sauce for seafood" (蒸鱼豉油) can replace the homemade sauce.

= Easy Shrimp Ceviche
serves: 4
time: Prep 15 min · Cook 5 min, plus 20 min in lime juice
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Keonar | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
- ½ medium red onion, diced
- 2 serrano peppers, seeded, deveined and finely chopped
- ¼ cup chopped cilantro
- 6 medium limes, divided
- 1½ tsp salt, divided
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ lb raw shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 small avocado, diced
[Method]
- Combine the cucumber, tomatoes, onion, serranos and cilantro in a bowl. Add 1 tsp of the salt and the juice of 1 lime, mix gently and set aside.
- Squeeze the remaining 5 limes into another bowl and add the rest of the salt and the pepper.
- Bring a small pot of water to a boil. Drop in the shrimp for 45 seconds, then scoop them out with a strainer.
- Chop the partly cooked shrimp into small pieces and stir them into the seasoned lime juice. Let sit 20 minutes.
- Combine with the vegetable mixture and top with the avocado.
[Tips]
- Unlike fish ceviche, shrimp shouldn't sit in citrus for hours. The quick blanch gives them a firmer texture and lowers the risk that comes with raw seafood.

= Heavenly Halibut
serves: 4
time: Prep 5 min · Cook 10 min
temp: Broil
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Chellebelle | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 2 lb skinless halibut fillets
- ½ cup grated Parmesan
- ¼ cup butter, softened
- 3 tbsp mayonnaise
- 3 tbsp chopped green onions
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- ¼ tsp salt
- Dash of hot sauce
- Fresh herbs and lemon slices, to garnish (optional)
[Method]
- Set a rack about 6 inches below the broiler and heat it. Grease a rimmed baking sheet and lay the halibut on it.
- Broil about 8 minutes, until the fish flakes easily.
- Meanwhile, mix the Parmesan, butter, mayonnaise, green onions, lemon juice, salt and hot sauce.
- Spread the topping over the fish and broil about 2 minutes more, until bubbling and lightly browned.
- Garnish with herbs and lemon slices, if you like, and serve hot.

= Lemon Garlic Swordfish
serves: 2–4 (two 6–7 oz steaks)
time: Prep 20 min · Cook 10 min
temp: 400°F / 200°C
from: Chew Out Loud | chewoutloud.com
[Ingredients: Lemon garlic butter]
- 2 tbsp salted butter, softened
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh chives
- 2 tbsp minced garlic
- ⅛ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tbsp grated lemon zest
[Ingredients: Fish]
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 swordfish steaks, 1 inch thick (6–7 oz each)
- Kosher salt and black pepper
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C) with a rack in the middle. Stir the lemon garlic butter ingredients together in a small pan and set aside.
- Pat the swordfish dry and season both sides with salt and pepper.
- Heat the oil in a large ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the fish about 3 minutes without moving it, until browned underneath. Flip, turn off the burner and move the pan straight into the oven.
- Roast 5–6 minutes, until the center is just cooked through. Don't overcook it.
- About a minute before the fish is ready, set the lemon garlic butter over medium-high heat and stir until melted and bubbling. Pour it over the fish, along with any juices from the pan.

= Lemon-Garlic Shrimp and Grits
serves: 4
time: Prep 15 min · Cook 15 min
from: Food Network Kitchen | foodnetwork.com
[Ingredients]
- ¾ cup instant grits
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
- 1¼ lb medium shrimp, peeled and deveined, tails on
- 2 large garlic cloves, minced
- Pinch of cayenne (optional)
- Juice of ½ lemon, plus wedges to serve
- 2 tbsp roughly chopped fresh parsley
[Method]
- Bring 3 cups of water to a boil in a covered medium saucepan. Uncover and slowly whisk in the grits, 1 tsp salt and ½ tsp pepper. Turn the heat to medium-low and cook about 5 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick.
- Stir in the Parmesan and 1 tbsp of the butter, season to taste, then cover and keep warm off the heat.
- Season the shrimp with salt and pepper. Melt the remaining 2 tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the shrimp, garlic and cayenne and toss for 3–4 minutes, until the shrimp are pink.
- Take the pan off the heat and add 2 tbsp water, the lemon juice and the parsley. Toss to coat and season to taste.
- Spoon the grits into shallow bowls, top with the shrimp and pan sauce and serve with lemon wedges.

= Nobu-Style Miso Black Cod
serves: 4
time: Prep 10 min, plus 2–3 days marinating · Cook 10–14 min
temp: Broil, or 400°F / 200°C
from: The Kitchn (adapted from Nobu: The Cookbook) | thekitchn.com
[Ingredients]
- ¼ cup sake
- ¼ cup mirin
- ¼ cup white miso
- 3 tbsp sugar
- 4 black cod (sablefish) fillets, 4–6 oz each, center-cut if possible
- Kosher salt
- Cooking spray, or 2 tbsp vegetable or canola oil
[Method]
- **Two to three days ahead:** Bring the sake and mirin to a boil in a small saucepan over high heat and let it bubble for 20 seconds to cook off the alcohol. Turn the heat to low, whisk in the miso and sugar until dissolved, then cool to room temperature.
- Pat the fillets dry and lay them snugly in a zip-top bag or container. Pour in the marinade, turn to coat, seal and refrigerate for 2–3 days.
- When ready to cook, lift the fish out and wipe off the excess marinade with your fingers, leaving a thin film (don't rinse it). Season lightly with salt. Cook by either method below.
- **Broiler:** Set a rack 6–8 inches below the broiler and heat it. Cover a wire rack with foil, set it on a baking sheet and spray the foil. Lay the fish skin-side down and broil 8–12 minutes, checking often and rotating the pan, until it flakes and the top is deep golden with charred spots.
- **Sear and bake:** Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Heat the oil in a 12-inch ovenproof skillet (cast iron is best) over medium-high until it just starts to smoke. Add the fish skin-side up and sear 2–3 minutes, until browned and blackened in spots. Flip with a thin metal spatula, sear 2–3 minutes more, then bake 5–10 minutes until opaque and flaky.
- Lift onto plates with a thin spatula and pull out any pin bones before serving.
[Tips]
- The sugar in the marinade scorches quickly, so stay close to the broiler.

= Perfect Ten Baked Cod
serves: 4
time: Prep 10 min · Cook 25 min
temp: 400°F / 200°C
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Pam | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 4 tbsp butter, divided
- ½ sleeve buttery round crackers (such as Ritz), crushed
- 1 lb thick-cut cod loin
- ¼ cup dry white wine
- Juice of ½ medium lemon
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
- 1 tbsp chopped green onion
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
- Microwave 2 tbsp of the butter in a bowl for about 30 seconds, until melted, and stir in the crushed crackers.
- Put the remaining 2 tbsp butter in a 7 × 11 baking dish and melt it in the oven, 1–3 minutes. Add the cod and turn it to coat in the butter.
- Bake 10 minutes. Pour the wine and lemon juice over the fish, top with the cracker mixture and bake about 10 minutes more, until opaque and flaky.
- Sprinkle with the parsley and green onion and serve with lemon wedges.

= Seared Ahi Tuna Steaks
serves: 2
time: Prep 5 min · Cook 5–8 min
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Bethany Joyful | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 2 ahi tuna steaks (5 oz each)
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp cayenne
- ½ tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp whole peppercorns
[Method]
- Pat the tuna dry and season both sides with the salt and cayenne.
- Melt the butter in a skillet over medium-high heat, add the oil and peppercorns and cook about 5 minutes, until the peppercorns soften and pop.
- Lay the tuna in the pan and sear 30 seconds to 1½ minutes per side, depending on how rare you like it.
- Slice ¼ inch thick to serve.
[Tips]
- If you like your tuna rare, buy sushi-grade.
