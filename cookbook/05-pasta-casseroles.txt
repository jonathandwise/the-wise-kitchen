= Broccoli, Rice, Cheese, and Chicken Casserole
serves: 8
time: Prep 15 min · Cook 30 min
temp: 350°F / 175°C
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Heather | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 2 cups water
- 2 cups uncooked instant rice
- 2 cans (10 oz each) chunk chicken, drained
- 1 package (16 oz) frozen chopped broccoli
- 1 lb processed cheese (such as Velveeta), cubed
- 1 can (10.75 oz) condensed cream of mushroom soup
- 1 can (10.75 oz) condensed cream of chicken soup
- 1 cup milk
- ¼ cup butter, cubed
- 1 small white onion, chopped
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
- Bring the water to a boil in a saucepan, stir in the rice, cover and take it off the heat. Let stand 5 minutes.
- Combine the rice, chicken, broccoli, cheese, both soups, milk, butter and onion in a 9 × 13 baking dish.
- Bake 30–35 minutes, until the cheese has melted, stirring halfway through so it melts evenly.
[Tips]
- It halves well. The author uses just one can of cream of mushroom soup for a half batch.

= Chicken Mushroom Tortellini Bake
makes: one 9 × 13 pan
time: Prep 10 min · Cook 35 min
temp: 350°F / 175°C
from: Let's Dish | letsdishrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 1 package (20 oz) fresh cheese tortellini
- 3 tbsp butter
- 3 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cubed
- 8 oz sliced fresh mushrooms
- 3 green onions, finely chopped
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1¾ cups chicken broth
- ¾ cup sour cream
- Salt and pepper
- 1½ cups shredded mozzarella or Monterey Jack
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9 × 13 baking dish.
- Melt the butter in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat and brown the chicken for 3–4 minutes. Add the mushrooms, green onions and garlic and cook until the chicken is cooked through and the mushrooms are golden.
- Sprinkle the flour over everything, stir well and cook for 1–2 minutes. Stir in the broth and cook, stirring, until thickened.
- Stir in the sour cream, season with salt and pepper, then fold in the uncooked tortellini.
- Spread the mixture in the dish and top with the cheese. Cover with foil and bake 25 minutes, then uncover and bake 10 minutes more, until bubbling and golden at the edges.

= Cottage Cheese Alfredo
serves: 4
time: Prep 10 min · Total 25 min
from: Delish · recipe by Brooke Caison | delish.com
note: Bookmarked as "Skinny Alfredo." A lighter fettuccine Alfredo: the creamy sauce is cottage cheese and milk blended smooth, with baby spinach and plenty of Parmesan, so it's lower in fat and high in protein.
[Ingredients]
- Kosher salt
- 8 oz dry fettuccine
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1¼ cups low-fat cottage cheese
- 1 cup 2% milk
- 1 oz Parmesan, shredded (about ⅓ cup packed)
- 2 small garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
- 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 3 oz baby spinach
- Crushed red pepper flakes
[Method]
- Cook the fettuccine in a large pot of boiling salted water, stirring now and then, until very al dente, about 1 minute less than the package says. Save ½ cup of the pasta water, then drain. Toss the pasta with the oil in a large bowl.
- Meanwhile, blend the cottage cheese, milk, Parmesan, garlic, butter, flour and 1 tsp salt in a blender or food processor until smooth.
- Pour the sauce into a large skillet and bring it to a simmer over medium heat. Cook, stirring often with a heatproof spatula, until thickened, 8–10 minutes. Stir in the spinach until it wilts.
- Add the pasta to the skillet and toss to coat, loosening the sauce with some of the pasta water if it gets too thick.
- Divide among bowls and sprinkle with red pepper flakes.

= World's Best Lasagna
serves: 12
time: Prep 30 min · Cook 2 hr 30 min
temp: 375°F / 190°C
from: Allrecipes · recipe by John Chandler | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients: Meat sauce]
- 1 lb sweet Italian sausage
- ¾ lb lean ground beef
- ½ cup minced onion
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 2 cans (6.5 oz each) tomato sauce
- 2 cans (6 oz each) tomato paste
- ½ cup water
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 4 tbsp chopped fresh parsley, divided
- 1½ tsp dried basil
- 1½ tsp salt, divided
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp fennel seeds
- ¼ tsp black pepper
[Ingredients: Noodles and cheese]
- 12 lasagna noodles
- 16 oz ricotta
- 1 egg
- ¾ lb mozzarella, sliced
- ¾ cup grated Parmesan
[Method]
- Brown the sausage, beef, onion and garlic in a Dutch oven over medium heat.
- Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, tomato paste and water. Season with the sugar, 2 tbsp of the parsley, the basil, 1 tsp of the salt, the Italian seasoning, fennel seeds and pepper. Cover and simmer about 1½ hours, stirring occasionally.
- Boil the noodles in lightly salted water for 8–10 minutes. Drain and rinse with cold water.
- Mix the ricotta with the egg, the remaining 2 tbsp parsley and ½ tsp salt.
- Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
- Spread 1½ cups of the sauce in a 9 × 13 baking dish. Lay 6 noodles lengthwise on top, slightly overlapping. Spread with half the ricotta mixture, then a third of the mozzarella. Spoon over 1½ cups sauce and sprinkle with ¼ cup Parmesan.
- Repeat the layers, then finish with the rest of the mozzarella and Parmesan.
- Cover with foil (spray it, or tent it so it doesn't touch the cheese) and bake 25 minutes. Uncover and bake 25 minutes more.
- Rest 15 minutes before cutting so it sets.
[Tips]
- The recipe's video uses three layers of 4 noodles that don't overlap. Done that way, use 1 cup sauce and a third of the ricotta per layer.
