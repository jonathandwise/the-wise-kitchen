= Best Green Bean Casserole
makes: one 1½-quart casserole
time: Prep 10 min · Cook 15 min
temp: 350°F / 175°C
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Jan | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 2 cans (14.5 oz each) green beans, drained well
- 1 can (10.5 oz) condensed cream of mushroom soup
- 1 cup shredded Cheddar, divided
- 1 can (6 oz) French-fried onions
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
- Mix the green beans and soup in a large microwave-safe bowl and microwave on High 3–5 minutes, until warm.
- Stir in ½ cup of the Cheddar and microwave 2–3 minutes more, until hot.
- Spread in a 1½-quart casserole dish, sprinkle with the remaining Cheddar and top with the fried onions.
- Bake about 10 minutes, until bubbling at the edges and the onions are lightly browned. Watch the onions near the end so they don't burn.
[Tips]
- A 16-oz bag of frozen cut green beans, thawed and drained, can replace the cans.
- For extra crunch, hold back a handful of onions and add them for the last few minutes.
- To make ahead, assemble without the onions up to a day early and refrigerate. Add the onions just before baking and allow a little extra time.

= Best Mashed Potatoes
serves: 4
time: Prep 15 min · Cook 15 min
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Esmee Williams | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 2 lb baking potatoes, peeled and quartered
- 3 garlic cloves, peeled (optional)
- 1 cup milk
- 2 tbsp butter
- Salt and black pepper
[Method]
- Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the potatoes (and garlic, if using), turn the heat to medium and simmer about 15 minutes, until easily pierced with a fork.
- When the potatoes are nearly done, warm the milk and butter in a small saucepan over low heat until the butter melts.
- Drain well and return the potatoes to the hot pot for 1–2 minutes to steam off excess moisture.
- Mash, adding the warm milk gradually until smooth and creamy (you may not need it all). Season, and top with extra butter if you like.
[Tips]
- Stir in up to 1 cup shredded Cheddar or Parmesan, or top with crispy fried onions or crumbled bacon.
- They can be made up to 2 days ahead. Reheat with a splash of milk and a little butter.

= Classic Eggplant Parmesan
serves: 6
time: Prep 50 min · Bake 30 min
temp: 400°F / 200°C
from: A Simple Palate | asimplepalate.com
[Ingredients: Eggplant]
- 2 medium to large eggplants
- ½ cup all-purpose flour
- 3 large eggs (or 4 smaller)
- ¼ cup olive oil, for frying
[Ingredients: Breadcrumbs]
- 6 slices bread, or 1½ cups store-bought breadcrumbs
- ½ cup shredded Parmesan
- 2 tsp dried Italian herb blend
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
[Ingredients: Toppings]
- 1½ cups marinara (up to 2½ cups if you like it saucy)
- 1 lb mozzarella
- ⅓ cup shredded Parmesan
- 1–2 tbsp chopped fresh basil
- 1 tsp chopped fresh oregano
[Method]
- Trim the eggplants and slice them into ¼–½-inch rounds.
- **Breadcrumbs:** Toast the bread and pulse it into crumbs in a food processor. Mix with the Parmesan, Italian herbs, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
- Set up three shallow bowls: flour, beaten eggs and the crumbs. Stir a pinch of salt into the flour and the eggs.
- Coat each slice in flour, then egg, then press it into the crumbs.
- Cook the slices by one of these methods:
  - **Pan-fry:** 2–3 minutes per side in 1–2 tbsp oil at a time, until golden. Drain on paper towels.
  - **Bake (less oil):** arrange on a sheet pan brushed with 1–2 tbsp oil and bake at 350°F (175°C) for about 25 minutes, until golden.
- Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Spread a generous layer of marinara over the bottom of a large baking dish.
- Layer the eggplant, mozzarella, a few spoonfuls of marinara and some Parmesan, then repeat once more.
- Bake uncovered 30 minutes, until bubbling and browned. Scatter with basil and oregano and serve.

= Gamja Salad (Korean Potato Salad)
makes: about 2 lb
time: Cook about 20 min, plus chilling
from: Maangchi | maangchi.com
[Ingredients]
- 1 lb russet potatoes, peeled and cut into ½-inch pieces
- 3 hard-boiled eggs, peeled
- 1 English cucumber, seeded and chopped
- Kosher salt
- ¼ tsp ground white pepper (or black)
- 2 tbsp rice syrup (or sugar)
- ½ cup mayonnaise
[Method]
- Toss the cucumber with ¼ tsp salt and let it sit 5 minutes, then squeeze out the water.
- Separate the egg yolks from the whites and chop the whites. Press 1 yolk through a sieve to use as a garnish; keep the other 2.
- Rinse the potatoes in cold water to remove excess starch. Put them in a heavy pot with ½ cup water and a pinch of salt and cook over medium-high heat for 20 minutes.
- Drain off any water, return the potatoes to the pot, cover and leave over very low heat for 1–2 minutes to dry out.
- Mash the hot potatoes for 30 seconds, then mash in the 2 reserved yolks and the chopped whites.
- Mix in the cucumber, pepper and rice syrup, then the mayonnaise. Let it cool, then refrigerate. Serve cold, garnished with the sieved yolk.
[Tips]
- Makes a great sandwich: a scoop on lettuce between slices of toasted bread.

= Garlic Mashed Potatoes
serves: 6
time: Prep 10 min · Cook 15 min
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Lorna Schmalz | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 8 potatoes, peeled and quartered
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp salt, for the cooking water
- ⅓ cup unsalted butter
- ½ cup milk
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- Pinch of ground white pepper
[Method]
- Put the potatoes, garlic and 1 tbsp salt in a saucepan with water to cover by 1 inch. Bring to a boil over high heat, then simmer over medium-low 10–12 minutes, until fork-tender.
- Drain, return the potatoes to the hot pan and stir about 1 minute to dry them out.
- Stir in the butter a piece at a time, letting each melt before adding more.
- Mash in the milk until smooth, then stir in the kosher salt and white pepper. Top with extra butter if you like.
[Tips]
- Adding the butter before the milk keeps them creamier for longer with less liquid, and cooking the garlic with the potatoes mellows it.
- Russets work but are starchier and less creamy. A spoonful of sour cream and some chives are good additions.
- **Roasted garlic version:** Cut the top off a whole bulb, drizzle with olive oil, wrap in foil and roast at 400°F (200°C) for about 40 minutes. Squeeze the soft cloves into the potatoes and mash.

= Italian Sausage Stuffed Zucchini
serves: 4
time: Prep 10 min · Total 35 min
temp: 350°F / 175°C
from: Delish · recipe by Lindsay Funston | delish.com
note: Zucchini boats filled with seared chicken sausage, Italian breadcrumbs, basil and two cheeses. Hearty enough to be dinner.
[Ingredients]
- 4 large zucchini, halved lengthwise
- 2 tsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 4 chicken sausage links, thinly sliced
- ½ cup Italian breadcrumbs
- ¾ cup shredded mozzarella, divided
- ½ cup shredded fontina
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- ¼ cup chopped fresh basil
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Score the flesh of each zucchini half in a crosshatch, as you would to dice an avocado, and scoop it out into a large bowl.
- Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the sausage and cook until seared, about 6 minutes.
- Transfer the sausage to the large bowl and add the breadcrumbs, ½ cup of the mozzarella, the fontina, garlic and basil. Mix well.
- Spoon the filling into the zucchini shells and top with the remaining ¼ cup mozzarella.
- Bake until the zucchini is tender and the cheese is golden, about 15 minutes.

= Magic Potatoes
time: Bake 2 hr
temp: 350°F / 175°C
from: Guga Foods on YouTube (recipe from Nate From The Internet) | youtube.com
[Ingredients]
- 1½ lb potatoes, diced
- 1 tsp finely chopped fresh rosemary
- 1 tsp finely chopped fresh thyme
- 1 cup grated Gruyère
- 1 cup grated Parmesan
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 1 tsp chopped fresh parsley
- Salt and black pepper
- Butter, for the dish
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and generously butter a baking dish.
- In a large bowl, toss the potatoes with salt, pepper, the rosemary and thyme, and most of the Gruyère and Parmesan (save a handful for the top).
- Spread the potatoes in the dish and pour the cream over them.
- Bake for 2 hours.
- Scatter the reserved cheese over the top and slide it under the broiler until browned and crusty.
- Sprinkle with parsley and serve.

= Pesto Spaghetti Squash
serves: 6
time: Prep 25 min · Cook 1 hr 5 min
temp: 400°F / 200°C
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Shyla Lane | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 1 spaghetti squash, halved lengthwise and seeded
- 3 tbsp butter, divided
- 1 onion, sliced
- 4 white mushrooms, sliced
- 1 cup chopped kale, stems removed
- 1 tsp garlic salt
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes
- 2 tbsp prepared pesto
- 1 tsp olive oil
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and grease a baking sheet.
- Bake the squash skin-side down about 1 hour, until cooked through. Cool 10 minutes, then scrape the flesh into strands with a fork.
- Melt 1 tbsp of the butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat and cook the onion until it starts to turn translucent. Stir in the mushrooms and kale and turn the heat to medium-low.
- Add the squash, the remaining 2 tbsp butter, the garlic salt, Italian seasoning and red pepper flakes and cook 2 minutes.
- Transfer to a large bowl and stir in the pesto and olive oil, then the Parmesan.

= Traditional Sweet Potato Casserole
serves: 16
time: Prep 15 min · Cook 40 min
temp: 375°F / 190°C
from: MyRecipes · recipe by Alison Ashton | myrecipes.com
note: The holiday standby: mashed sweet potatoes with brown sugar and vanilla, finished with pecans and toasted marshmallows.
[Ingredients]
- 2½ lb sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into 1-inch cubes
- ¾ cup packed brown sugar
- ¼ cup butter, softened
- 1½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp vanilla extract
- ½ cup finely chopped pecans, divided
- Cooking spray
- 2 cups miniature marshmallows
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
- Put the sweet potatoes in a Dutch oven and cover with cold water. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer until very tender, about 15 minutes. Drain and let cool slightly.
- Transfer the potatoes to a large bowl, add the brown sugar, butter, salt and vanilla, and mash with a potato masher until smooth. Fold in ¼ cup of the pecans.
- Spread the mixture evenly in an 11 × 7-inch baking dish coated with cooking spray. Sprinkle with the remaining ¼ cup pecans and top with the marshmallows.
- Bake until golden, about 25 minutes.
[Tips]
- Rather have a crunchy brown-sugar and pecan crumble than marshmallows? See the Yummy Sweet Potato Casserole.

= Yummy Sweet Potato Casserole
makes: one 9 × 13 casserole
time: Prep 15 min · Cook 45 min
temp: 325°F / 165°C
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Tina B | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients: Filling]
- 4 cups peeled, cubed sweet potatoes
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ cup milk
- 4 tbsp butter, softened
- ½ tsp vanilla extract
- ½ tsp salt
[Ingredients: Pecan topping]
- ½ cup packed brown sugar
- ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
- 3 tbsp butter, softened
- ½ cup chopped pecans
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 325°F (165°C).
- Cover the sweet potatoes with water in a saucepan and cook over medium-high heat 10–15 minutes, until fork-tender. Drain and put them in a large bowl.
- Mash with a fork, then mix in the eggs. Add the sugar, milk, butter, vanilla and salt and mix until smooth. Spread in a 9 × 13 baking dish.
- **Topping:** Mix the brown sugar and flour, then cut in the butter with a pastry cutter until it looks like coarse crumbs. Stir in the pecans and sprinkle over the sweet potatoes.
- Bake about 30 minutes, until the topping is lightly browned.
[Tips]
- An immersion blender makes the filling extra creamy.
- Make the filling up to 2 days ahead and keep the topping separate in the fridge. Add the topping just before baking, and allow a few extra minutes if the dish is cold.
- Leftovers keep 3–4 days. Reheat covered at 350°F for 20–25 minutes.
