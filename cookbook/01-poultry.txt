= Air Fryer Chicken Tenders
serves: 4 (3 tenders each)
time: Prep 5 min · Cook 30 min in batches
temp: 400°F / 200°C air fryer
from: Skinnytaste | skinnytaste.com
[Ingredients]
- 12 chicken tenders (1¼ lb)
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- Black pepper
- ½ cup seasoned breadcrumbs
- ½ cup seasoned panko
- Olive oil spray
- Lemon wedges, to serve
[Method]
- Season the chicken with the salt and some pepper.
- Put the eggs in one shallow bowl and mix the breadcrumbs and panko in another.
- Dip each tender in egg, then in the crumbs, shaking off the excess. Spray both sides generously with oil.
- Preheat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C).
- Cook in batches, 5–6 minutes per side, until golden, crisp and cooked through. Serve with lemon wedges.
[Tips]
- Use gluten-free breadcrumbs and panko for a gluten-free version.

= Avocado Smothered Chicken
serves: 6
time: Prep 10 min · Cook 30 min
temp: 375°F / 190°C
from: Easy Family Recipes | easyfamilyrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 2 lb thin-cut boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 2 large avocados
- ¼ cup oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes, diced
- 2 tbsp finely diced red onion
- 2 tbsp chopped cilantro
- ½ tsp salt, divided
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- ½ cup shredded Monterey Jack
[Ingredients: Sweet chili drizzle (or use store-bought sweet chili sauce)]
- 1–2 tbsp chili sauce
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp rice wine vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp chopped cilantro
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Lay the chicken flat in a large baking dish and season with half the salt and all the pepper.
- Mash the avocados with the sun-dried tomatoes, red onion, cilantro and the rest of the salt, leaving some chunks.
- Spread the avocado mixture over each piece of chicken and top with the cheese.
- Cover with foil and bake 25–35 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through (165°F / 74°C). For browned cheese, uncover and broil for the last 3–5 minutes.
- Whisk the drizzle ingredients together, using 1 tbsp chili sauce for mild or 2 for spicy.
- Serve over rice or sautéed spinach, drizzled with the sauce.
[Tips]
- To freeze, assemble through step 3, wrap the dish well and freeze unbaked. Thaw completely before baking.
- Avocado browns in the fridge, so freeze leftovers rather than refrigerating them.

= Baked Salsa Chicken
serves: 4
time: Prep 5 min · Cook 33 min
temp: 375°F / 190°C
from: The Blond Cook | theblondcook.com
[Ingredients]
- 2 large boneless, skinless chicken breasts, halved horizontally into thin cutlets
- 1½ tbsp taco seasoning
- 1½ cups jarred salsa
- 1 cup shredded Mexican cheese blend
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and grease a baking dish.
- Rub the chicken all over with the taco seasoning, lay it in the dish and spoon the salsa over the top.
- Bake uncovered for 30 minutes, until cooked through (165°F / 74°C).
- Scatter over the cheese and bake 2–3 minutes more, until melted. Sprinkle with cilantro.
[Tips]

= Chipotle-Style Chicken
time: Overnight marinating · Cook about 8 min
from: CookingwithChris on YouTube | youtube.com
[Ingredients]
- 4–6 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- Kosher salt, for sprinkling
[Ingredients: Marinade]
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 2 tbsp olive or sunflower oil
- 1 can (7 oz) chipotle peppers in adobo: 4 of the peppers plus all of the sauce
- ¾ cup water
[Method]
- Put all the marinade ingredients in a blender, using just 4 chipotles and all the adobo sauce (keep the leftover peppers for another use). Blend on low to medium for 1–2 minutes, until smooth.
- Put the chicken in a gallon zip-top bag, pour in the marinade, press out as much air as possible and seal. Refrigerate overnight, or at least 8 hours.
- Heat a cast-iron skillet (or grill) over medium to medium-high heat. Add the chicken and sprinkle the tops with kosher salt.
- Cook 4–5 minutes, until deeply charred underneath, then flip and cook about 3 minutes more, until 165°F (74°C) inside. Pulling it at 163°F lets carryover heat finish the job.
- Slice each thigh lengthwise into strips, then cut across into ¾-inch cubes, just like the restaurant does.

= Cornish Game Hens with Garlic and Rosemary
serves: 4 (one hen each)
time: Prep 20 min · Cook 1 hr
temp: 450°F, then 350°F (230°C, then 175°C)
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Moonandback | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 4 Cornish game hens
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- Salt and pepper
- 1 lemon, quartered
- 8 sprigs fresh rosemary, divided
- 24 garlic cloves
- ⅓ cup white wine
- ⅓ cup low-sodium chicken broth
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C).
- Rub the hens with 1 tbsp of the oil and season lightly. Put a lemon quarter and a rosemary sprig in each cavity. Set the hens in a large, heavy roasting pan and scatter the garlic around them.
- Roast 25 minutes. Meanwhile, whisk the wine, broth and remaining 2 tbsp oil together.
- Turn the oven down to 350°F (175°C). Pour the wine mixture over the hens and roast about 25 minutes more, basting every 10 minutes, until golden and 165°F (74°C) near the bone.
- Move the hens to a platter, tipping the juices from the cavities back into the pan. Discard the lemon and rosemary and tent the hens with foil.
- Pour the pan juices and garlic into a saucepan and boil about 6 minutes, until reduced to a sauce.
- Spoon the sauce and roasted garlic over the hens and garnish with the remaining rosemary.
[Tips]
- The author serves these with crusty garlic bread and a light Chianti.

= Garlic Herb Butter Roast Chicken
serves: 4
time: Prep 10 min · Cook 1 hr 15 min
temp: 430°F / 220°C (400°F / 200°C fan)
from: Cafe Delites | cafedelites.com
[Ingredients: Chicken]
- 1 whole chicken, about 4 lb (1.8 kg), at room temperature, giblets removed
- ¼ cup unsalted butter, melted
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- ¼ cup dry white wine (optional)
- 1 lemon, halved
- Salt and pepper
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 whole head garlic, halved crosswise
- 3 sprigs fresh rosemary
[Ingredients: Pan gravy (optional)]
- 2 tbsp chicken fat skimmed from the drippings
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- Remaining pan juices
- Salt and pepper
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 430°F (220°C), or 400°F (200°C) on fan. Line a baking tray with foil or grease a roasting pan.
- Trim any excess fat from the chicken and pat it completely dry.
- Drizzle the oil, melted butter, wine and the juice of half the lemon over the bird, working some under the skin and into the cavity. Season generously inside and out with salt and pepper, then scatter over the parsley.
- Rub the minced garlic all over the skin.
- Fill the cavity with the halved garlic head, the rosemary and the squeezed lemon half. Tie the legs together.
- Roast breast-side up for 1 hr 15–20 min, basting once halfway, until the juices from the thigh run clear (165°F / 74°C in the thickest part).
- Baste again, then broil for 2–3 minutes to finish browning the skin.
- Tent with foil and rest 10 minutes. Serve with the pan juices and the remaining lemon half cut into wedges.
[Method: Pan gravy]
- Pour the pan juices into a jug or fat separator and let the fat rise for a minute.
- Spoon 2 tbsp of the fat into a small saucepan over medium heat, whisk in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
- Gradually whisk in the broth, then the rest of the dark pan juices.
- Simmer 2–3 minutes, stirring, until it coats a spoon. Season to taste.
[Tips]
- Not enough fat for the gravy? Make up the difference with melted butter. If it gets too thick, loosen it with a splash of broth.
- Leftover meat, off the bone, keeps 4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen.

= Herb-Brined Cornish Game Hens
serves: 8 (4 hens)
time: Prep 35 min, plus 1–2 hr brining · Roast 35–40 min
temp: 450°F / 230°C
from: Taste of Home | tasteofhome.com
[Ingredients: Brine]
- ⅔ cup kosher salt
- ¼ cup packed brown sugar
- 12 whole peppercorns
- 5 fresh sage leaves
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 sprig fresh thyme
- 1 sprig fresh rosemary
- 1 quart water, plus 1½ quarts cold water
- 2 large (turkey-size) oven roasting bags
- 4 Cornish game hens (about 20 oz each)
[Ingredients: Herb butter]
- 14 whole peppercorns
- 2 garlic cloves
- ¾ cup butter, softened
- 3 tbsp plus 1 tsp olive oil, divided
- ⅓ cup packed fresh parsley sprigs
- 3 tbsp fresh sage leaves
- 1 tbsp fresh rosemary leaves
- 2 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
[Ingredients: Vegetables]
- 2 lb Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
- 2 small red onions, cut into wedges
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp coarsely ground pepper
[Method]
- **Brine:** Boil the salt, brown sugar, 12 peppercorns, herbs, garlic and 1 quart water, stirring until dissolved. Off the heat, add the 1½ quarts cold water and let cool.
- Nest one roasting bag inside the other, add the hens and the cooled brine, press out the air and seal. Set in a roasting pan and refrigerate 1–2 hours, turning occasionally. Drain and pat the hens dry.
- **Herb butter:** Pulse the 14 peppercorns and 2 garlic cloves in a food processor until coarsely chopped. Add the butter, 3 tbsp oil and the herbs and process until smooth.
- Loosen the skin on each hen and spread half the herb butter underneath. Pin the skin under the breast with toothpicks, tie the legs and rub the rest of the butter on top.
- Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C). In a 15 × 10 × 1-inch pan, toss the Brussels sprouts and onions with the remaining 1 tsp oil, ½ tsp salt and ½ tsp pepper and spread them out.
- Set the hens breast-side up on the vegetables. Roast 35–40 minutes, until a thermometer in the breast reads 165°F (74°C). If they brown too fast, tent loosely with foil.
- Rest the hens under foil for 10 minutes, then carve and serve with the vegetables.

= Killer Chicken Thighs
serves: 8
time: Prep 15 min, plus marinating · Bake 30 min
temp: 450°F / 230°C
from: Craving Tasty | cravingtasty.com
[Ingredients]
- 8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 4 lb)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 4 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tbsp lemon or lime juice
- 5 tbsp maple syrup or honey
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 2 tsp kosher salt
[Method]
- Mix everything except the chicken in a large bowl or zip-top bag. Add the thighs and turn to coat. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes to 2 hours, or ideally overnight.
- Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C).
- Put the chicken, skin-side up, in a baking dish with all the marinade. Bake uncovered 25–30 minutes, until 165°F (74°C) inside, basting with the pan juices after 15–20 minutes.
- For crisper skin, broil for the last 2–3 minutes. Watch closely, since the sugars burn quickly.

= Oven-Baked Chicken and Rice
serves: 6
time: Prep 10 min · Cook 1 hr
temp: 350°F / 180°C
from: Cafe Delites | cafedelites.com
[Ingredients: Chicken]
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp light brown sugar
- 1 tsp dried oregano (or dried parsley)
- 1 tsp paprika, sweet or hot
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp salt
- ¼ tsp cracked black pepper
- 6 skinless chicken thighs, bone-in or boneless
[Ingredients: Rice]
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 4 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- ¼ tsp cracked black pepper
- 1½ cups hot chicken broth
- 1¼ cups hot water
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1½ cups long-grain white rice
[Ingredients: Garlic mushrooms (optional)]
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 14 oz button mushrooms, quartered
- ¼ cup chopped chives or green onions, divided
- ¼ tsp each salt and pepper
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 350°F (180°C).
- In a shallow bowl, mix the oil, brown sugar, oregano, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, thyme, salt and pepper. Turn the chicken in it until evenly coated. (If you have time, marinate overnight.)
- Grease a 10 × 15-inch baking dish. Add the onion, garlic, oil, salt, pepper, hot broth, hot water and butter, and stir until the butter melts. Stir in the rice.
- Lay the chicken over the middle of the rice. Cover tightly with foil and bake 30 minutes. Uncover, spray the chicken with oil and bake another 25–30 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the rice is tender. Broil 3–5 minutes to brown the top.
- Lift off the chicken, stir the rice, return the chicken, then cover and rest 10 minutes; the rice finishes steaming as it sits.
- **Mushrooms (optional, while it rests):** Melt the butter in a pan over medium-high heat. Cook the garlic 30 seconds, add the mushrooms and cook until soft. Stir in 2 tbsp of the chives and season.
- Fluff the rice and fold in the mushrooms. Return the chicken, sprinkle with the rest of the chives and the parsley, and serve.
[Tips]
- Use skinless thighs. Skin-on thighs leave the rice gluey and undercooked.
- Bone-in breasts or drumsticks work in the same time.
- Stick to long-grain, basmati or jasmine rice. Short-grain, risotto and instant rice won't cook right.
- Center the chicken so all the rice sits under liquid and cooks evenly.

= Pressure Cooker Teriyaki Chicken
serves: 8
time: Prep 20 min · Cook 20 min
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Lesliesami | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 1 can (20 oz) crushed pineapple
- 1 cup chicken stock
- ¾ cup brown sugar
- ¾ cup reduced-sodium soy sauce
- ¼ cup cider vinegar
- 2 tbsp ground ginger
- 2 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 3 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- 2 tbsp cold water
[Method]
- Stir the pineapple, stock, brown sugar, soy sauce, vinegar, ginger, garlic powder and pepper together until the sugar dissolves. Put the chicken in the pressure cooker and stir in the sauce to coat.
- Lock the lid and bring up to High pressure (on a stovetop cooker, lower the heat once it reaches pressure). Cook 20 minutes.
- Mix the cornstarch with the cold water.
- Quick-release the pressure. Lift the chicken onto a serving dish with a slotted spoon and cover with foil.
- Whisk the cornstarch mixture into the sauce, bring it to a boil (use Sauté on an electric cooker) and cook about 3 minutes, stirring often, until thickened. Spoon over the chicken.

= Stuffed Chicken Parmesan
serves: 3
temp: 350°F / 180°C
from: Tasty | tasty.co
note: Chicken Parmesan with a pocket of mozzarella in the middle: breaded, pan-fried until golden, then baked in tomato sauce.
[Ingredients]
- 3 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- Salt, to taste
- 1 cup mozzarella (100 g)
- 2 cups flour (250 g)
- 6 eggs, beaten
- 2 cups bread crumbs (230 g)
- 1 cup oil (240 g), for frying
- 3 cups tomato sauce (780 g)
- ½ cup grated Parmesan (55 g)
- 2 tbsp fresh basil
[Method]
- Season the chicken with salt. Cut a deep pocket into the side of each breast without cutting all the way through.
- Fill the pockets evenly with the mozzarella, then press the edges together to seal.
- Set out the flour, beaten eggs and bread crumbs in three shallow bowls. Dredge each breast in flour and shake off the excess, keeping the pocket closed. Dip it in the egg, then coat it evenly with bread crumbs.
- Heat the oven to 350°F (180°C). Heat the oil in a large pan over medium heat and fry the chicken until golden brown on both sides.
- Spread two-thirds of the tomato sauce over the bottom of a baking dish and set the chicken on top. Cover with the rest of the sauce and sprinkle with the Parmesan and basil.
- Bake for 20 minutes.
[Tips]
- This was bookmarked as a Gfycat clip of Tasty's video. Gfycat has since shut down, so the recipe here comes from Tasty's own site.
- Large breasts may need longer in the oven. The chicken is safe once the thickest part reaches 165°F (74°C).

= Turkey Meatloaf
serves: 6
time: Prep 20 min · Cook 55 min
temp: 350°F / 175°C
from: Once Upon a Chef | onceuponachef.com
[Ingredients: Glaze]
- ⅔ cup ketchup
- ⅓ cup packed dark brown sugar
- 1½ tbsp apple cider vinegar
- ½ tsp Dijon mustard
[Ingredients: Meatloaf]
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium yellow onion, minced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1¼ tsp salt
- ¾ tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1½ tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1½ tbsp Dijon mustard
- 2 large eggs
- 2 lb ground turkey (93% lean)
- ¾ cup Italian-seasoned breadcrumbs
- Chopped fresh parsley, to garnish (optional)
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) with a rack in the middle. Line a rimmed baking sheet with heavy-duty foil and grease it.
- Whisk the glaze ingredients together in a small bowl.
- Cook the onion in the olive oil over medium heat for about 5 minutes, until soft. Add the garlic and cook 1–2 minutes more without browning. Let cool.
- In a large bowl, whisk the salt, pepper, paprika, thyme, Worcestershire, Dijon, eggs and ¼ cup of the glaze. Add the cooled onion, the turkey and the breadcrumbs and mix with your hands until evenly combined.
- Divide the mixture in half and shape each portion on the baking sheet into a loaf about 6 inches long, 4 inches wide and 1½ inches high.
- Spread the remaining glaze over the loaves, letting it run down the sides.
- Bake about 45 minutes, until cooked through (165°F / 74°C in the center). Scrape away any fat, slice and garnish with parsley.

= Adams Family Duck
makes: one whole duck
time: Prep 30 min · Cook 2 hr 15 min
temp: 350°F / 175°C
from: Allrecipes · recipe by The Magpie | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients: Duck and stuffing]
- 1 whole duck (about 5 lb), neck and giblets saved
- 1 can (6 oz) frozen orange juice concentrate, thawed, divided
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- Salt and black pepper
- 1 onion, coarsely chopped, divided
- 1 cup raisins
- 1 apple, peeled, cored and cut into chunks
- 1 large carrot, thickly sliced
[Ingredients: Basting mixture]
- ½ cup chicken broth
- ¼ cup lemon juice
- Salt and pepper
[Ingredients: Duck stock]
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, coarsely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 carrot, thickly sliced
- 1 cup chicken broth
- ½ cup lemon juice
- 1 cup orange juice
- 1 sprig fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt and pepper
[Ingredients: To finish]
- 1 tbsp sour cream
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Rinse the duck, trim the excess fat from around the cavity and prick the skin all over with a fork, without piercing the meat.
- Rub 1 tbsp of the orange juice concentrate over the skin and inside the cavity, then rub in the minced garlic and season inside and out with salt and pepper.
- Mix about 2 tbsp of the chopped onion with the raisins and apple and stuff the cavity loosely. Truss the duck and set it on a rack in a roasting pan. Scatter the rest of that onion and the carrot slices around it.
- **Basting mixture:** Whisk the remaining orange juice concentrate with the ½ cup broth, ¼ cup lemon juice, salt and pepper.
- Roast 1 hour, basting now and then. Pour off the rendered fat, then roast about 1 hour more, still basting, until the thigh reads 165°F (74°C) and the juices run clear. Save any leftover basting mixture.
- **Duck stock (while the duck roasts):** Brown the neck and giblets in the olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion, garlic and carrot and cook about 5 minutes, until the onion is translucent. Add the broth, lemon juice, orange juice, parsley, salt and pepper, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer 1 hour. Strain and set aside.
- Move the duck to a warm platter with the roasted vegetables, spoon the stuffing into a bowl and let the duck rest about 15 minutes.
- **Sauce:** Skim the fat from the roasting pan, add the leftover basting mixture and scrape up the browned bits over medium heat. Add the duck stock, bring to a boil and reduce by about half, stirring, about 10 minutes. Whisk in the sour cream until glossy and pour over the carved duck.

= Peking Duck
serves: 4
time: Prep 15 min, plus 2 hr to overnight marinating · Cook 1 hr 35 min
temp: 375°F, then 500°F (190°C, then 260°C)
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Allison | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients: Duck]
- 1 whole duck (about 4 lb), dressed
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- ½ tsp ground ginger
- ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
- ¼ tsp ground white pepper
- ⅛ tsp ground cloves
- 3 tbsp soy sauce, divided
- 1 tbsp honey
- 5 green onions, divided
[Ingredients: Plum sauce]
- ½ cup plum jam
- ¼ cup finely chopped chutney
- 1½ tsp sugar
- 1½ tsp distilled white vinegar
[Ingredients: To serve]
- 1 orange, sliced into rounds
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
[Method]
- Rinse the duck inside and out, pat dry and cut off the tail.
- Mix the cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, white pepper and cloves. Sprinkle 1 tsp of the mix inside the cavity.
- Stir 1 tbsp of the soy sauce into the rest of the spice mix and rub it all over the outside of the duck. Halve 1 green onion and tuck it into the cavity.
- Cover and refrigerate at least 2 hours, or overnight.
- Set the duck breast-side up on a rack in a wok or large pot over simmering water and steam 1 hour, topping up the water as it evaporates.
- Lift the duck out, letting the juices and green onion drain from the cavity.
- Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Put the duck breast-side up in a roasting pan, prick the skin all over with a fork and roast 30 minutes.
- Mix the remaining 2 tbsp soy sauce with the honey and brush it over the duck. Turn the oven up to 500°F (260°C) and roast about 5 minutes, until the skin is deep brown but not charred.
- **Plum sauce:** Stir together the plum jam, chutney, sugar and vinegar. Chop the remaining green onions.
- Serve the whole duck on a platter with the orange slices and parsley, with the plum sauce and green onions alongside.

= Roast Duck with Apple Dressing
serves: 4
time: Prep 10 min · Cook 1 hr 20 min
temp: 350°F / 175°C
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Debbie | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 1 whole duck (about 4 lb)
- Salt and pepper
- 1 tsp poultry seasoning
- ½ tbsp butter
- 3 tbsp chopped onion
- 5 celery stalks, chopped
- 3 cups peeled, cored and chopped apple
- 3 cups cornbread crumbs
- 1 tbsp olive oil
[Method]
- Rinse and dry the duck, then rub it inside and out with salt, pepper and the poultry seasoning.
- Melt the butter in a small skillet over medium heat and cook the onion and celery until tender. Mix with the apple and cornbread crumbs in a bowl to make the dressing, adding a splash of water if it seems dry.
- Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
- Stuff the duck with the dressing and sew the opening closed with kitchen twine. Rub the skin lightly with the olive oil and set the duck in a shallow roasting pan or 9 × 13 dish.
- Roast 60–80 minutes, until the thickest part reads 180°F (80°C).
