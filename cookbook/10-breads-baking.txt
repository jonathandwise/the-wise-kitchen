= Jay's Signature Pizza Crust
makes: one large pizza crust
time: Prep 30 min, plus 1 hr rising · Bake 15–20 min
temp: 425°F / 220°C
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Jason Sharp | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 1½ cups warm water (110°F / 43°C)
- 2¼ tsp active dry yeast
- ½ tsp brown sugar
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 3⅓ cups all-purpose flour, divided
[Method]
- Stir the warm water, yeast and brown sugar together in a large bowl and leave 10 minutes.
- Stir in the oil and salt, then mix in 2½ cups of the flour. Turn out onto a floured surface and knead, working in the remaining flour a little at a time, until the dough is no longer sticky. Put it in an oiled bowl.
- Cover with a towel and let rise about 1 hour, until doubled.
- Punch the dough down, shape it into a tight ball and let it relax 1 minute before rolling out.
- Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
- On a pizza stone, add toppings and bake right away. On a pan, oil the pan and let the dough rise 15–20 minutes before topping.
- Bake 15–20 minutes, until the cheese melts and the crust is golden.

= Naan
makes: 8 pieces
time: Prep 30 min · Rise 1 hr · Cook 10 min
from: Rasa Malaysia | rasamalaysia.com
[Ingredients]
- 1 tsp sugar
- ½ cup warm water
- 1 packet (¼ oz / 2¼ tsp) active dry yeast
- 300–350 g (2¼–2⅓ cups) all-purpose flour
- ½ cup plain yogurt
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp oil, plus more for the pan
- 3 tbsp melted butter or ghee
- Chopped fresh cilantro (optional)
[Method]
- Stir the sugar, warm water and yeast together and leave about 10 minutes, until foamy.
- Heap the flour on a work surface (start with 300 g) and make a well in the middle. Add the yeast mixture, yogurt, salt and 1 tbsp oil and knead about 10 minutes, until smooth and shiny.
- Cover with a damp cloth and leave in a warm place about 1 hour, until doubled.
- Divide into 8 pieces and roll each into a 6-inch round.
- Heat a cast-iron skillet over high heat and lightly oil it. Cook one naan at a time until it puffs and the underside has browned blisters, then flip and cook the other side.
- Brush with melted butter or ghee, sprinkle with cilantro and serve warm.
[Tips]
- Flour absorbs differently depending on the weather. Add flour beyond 300 g only if the dough is still sticky.
- The pan needs to be very hot to get those blisters.

= No-Knead Soft Pretzel Rolls
makes: 10 rolls
time: 8–10 hr rise + 4–5 hr proof · Bake 25 min
temp: 400°F / 204°C
from: Alexandra's Kitchen | alexandracooks.com
[Ingredients: Dough]
- 640 g (about 5 cups) all-purpose flour
- 2 g (½ tsp) instant yeast
- 13 g kosher salt (4 tsp Diamond Crystal, or 2 tsp Morton or fine sea salt)
- 12 g (1 tbsp) sugar
- 450 g (2 cups) cold water
- 14 g (1 tbsp) olive oil or neutral oil
[Ingredients: To finish]
- 115 g (½ cup) baking soda
- 8 cups water
- Flaky sea salt
- 6 tbsp melted butter
[Method]
- Whisk the flour, yeast, salt and sugar in a large bowl. Add the water and oil and stir into a shaggy, sticky dough, kneading briefly in the bowl until it comes together.
- Cover airtight and let rise until doubled, 8–10 hours.
- Turn the dough out onto a floured surface and divide into 10 pieces (about 112 g each).
- Shape each into a tight ball: pull the edges into the center, flip it seam-down, then drag it toward you with cupped hands to tighten the surface.
- Space the balls in a floured container or two 9 × 13 pans, cover and proof 4–5 hours, until doubled and very airy.
- Heat the oven to 400°F (204°C) and line two sheet pans with parchment.
- Bring the 8 cups of water to a simmer, lower the heat and stir in the baking soda. Keep it barely simmering.
- One at a time, lower a ball into the water top-side down, flip it over and back again, then lift it out with a spider. The whole dip should take no more than 30 seconds. Set it top-side up on a pan, five to a pan.
- Brush with butter, sprinkle generously with flaky salt and slash the tops twice with a razor or sharp knife. Bake 22–25 minutes, until deep brown.
- Brush with more butter straight out of the oven. Repeat with the second pan. Cool briefly, or completely if using for sandwiches.
[Tips]
- A kitchen scale makes this much more reliable.
- Active dry yeast: sprinkle it over the cold water and let it stand 15 minutes before mixing.
- Faster version (not tested by the author): use 2 tsp instant yeast and lukewarm water, rise 2–3 hours, then proof the shaped rolls at least 1 hour.
- After the first rise the dough can wait in the fridge; let shaped rolls proof at room temperature until very light before boiling.

= Quick and Easy Pizza Crust
makes: one 12-inch crust
time: Prep 10 min, plus 15 min standing · Bake 15–20 min
temp: 450°F / 230°C
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Chef Rider | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 1 cup warm water (110°F / 43°C)
- 1 packet (¼ oz) active dry yeast
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2½ cups bread flour
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp salt
- Sauce and toppings of your choice
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C) and lightly grease a pizza pan.
- Stir the yeast and sugar into the warm water and leave about 10 minutes, until creamy.
- Add the flour, oil and salt and beat until smooth, by hand or with a dough hook. Rest 5 minutes.
- Pat or roll the dough into a 12-inch round on a lightly floured surface and move it to the pan.
- Add sauce and toppings and bake 15–20 minutes, until golden. Cool 5 minutes before slicing.
[Tips]
- If you have a pizza stone, dust it with cornmeal and bake on that instead of the greased pan.

= Starbucks-Style Muesli Bread
makes: 1 loaf
time: Overnight rise · Bake 25–35 min
temp: 450°F / 230°C
from: The Chopping Block | thechoppingblock.com
[Ingredients]
- 1½ cups warm water
- 3¼ cups all-purpose flour
- 1½ tsp (½ tbsp) salt
- 2 tbsp sugar
- ¾ tbsp instant (fast-acting) yeast, about 1 packet
- ½ cup dried cranberries (Craisins)
- ½ cup mixed nuts, chopped
[Method]
- Stir the water, flour, salt, sugar and yeast together in a large bowl until no dry flour remains. The dough will be sticky; don't add more flour. Cover and refrigerate all day or overnight.
- When you're ready to bake, set a cast-iron skillet on the bottom rack and heat the oven to 450°F (230°C).
- Knead the dough on a lightly floured counter for 1 minute. Add the cranberries and nuts and knead a couple of minutes more, tucking them inside the dough so they don't scorch.
- Shape into a loaf on a parchment-lined baking sheet or baking stone. Slash the top 2–3 times, about ½ inch deep.
- Slide the bread into the oven, carefully pour hot water into the skillet and shut the door quickly. Keep the door closed so the steam stays in.
- Bake 25–35 minutes, until deep golden and hollow-sounding when tapped underneath. Cool completely on a rack before slicing.
[Tips]
- The wet, sticky dough is what gives this bread its open, chewy crumb.
- The steam from the skillet can burn you: wear an oven mitt and stand back as you pour.

= Strawberry Bread
makes: Two 9 × 5-inch loaves
time: Prep 20 min · Bake 45–50 min
temp: 350°F / 175°C
from: Allrecipes | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 2 cups fresh strawberries, sliced
- 1 tbsp sugar, for the berries
- 3⅛ cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 tbsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1¼ cups vegetable oil
- 4 large eggs, beaten
- 1¼ cups chopped pecans
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour two 9 × 5-inch loaf pans.
- Toss the strawberries with 1 tbsp sugar in a medium bowl.
- In a large bowl, mix the flour, 2 cups sugar, cinnamon, salt and baking soda.
- Stir the oil and eggs into the strawberries, then add them to the flour mixture and stir just until moistened. Fold in the pecans and divide between the pans.
- Bake 45–50 minutes, until a tester in the center comes out clean (check each loaf).
- Cool in the pans 10 minutes, then turn out and cool before slicing.

= 1-Minute Protein Mug Cake
serves: 1
time: 1 min in the microwave
from: Noel Deyzel on YouTube | youtube.com
[Ingredients]
- 30 g whey protein powder
- 1 tbsp flour
- ½ tsp baking powder
- 1 tbsp cocoa powder
- 1 egg
- 70 ml (just under ⅓ cup) milk
[Method]
- Lightly grease a large microwave-safe mug.
- Add the protein powder, flour, baking powder and cocoa and stir together.
- Whisk the egg and milk until slightly frothy, pour into the mug and stir until smooth.
- Microwave for 1 minute.
[Tips]
- Microwaves vary. If the center is still wet, give it another 15–20 seconds.

= Best Brownies
makes: 16 brownies
time: Prep 15 min · Bake 25–30 min
temp: 350°F / 175°C
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Angie | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients: Brownies]
- ½ cup butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- ⅓ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- ½ cup all-purpose flour
- ¼ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp baking powder
[Ingredients: Frosting]
- 3 tbsp butter, softened
- 3 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup confectioners' sugar
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour an 8-inch square pan.
- Melt the ½ cup butter in a large saucepan. Off the heat, stir in the sugar, eggs and vanilla, then beat in the cocoa, flour, salt and baking powder. Spread in the pan.
- Bake 25–30 minutes, until the top is dry and the edges pull away from the pan. Let cool briefly.
- **Frosting:** Stir the butter, confectioners' sugar, cocoa, honey and vanilla together until smooth, and spread over the brownies while they're still slightly warm.
[Tips]
- Fold ½ cup chopped walnuts, pecans or hazelnuts into the batter for a nutty version.

= Blueberry Cream Cheese Muffins
makes: 15 muffins
time: Prep 20 min · Bake 25 min
temp: 375°F / 190°C
from: Sugar Spun Run | sugarspunrun.com
[Ingredients: Muffins]
- ¼ cup (60 ml) vegetable oil
- 4 tbsp (57 g) unsalted butter, melted
- 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- 1 large egg plus 1 large egg white, at room temperature
- ½ cup (120 ml) buttermilk
- 1½ tsp vanilla extract
- 1¾ cups (220 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 2 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt
- 1½ cups (210 g) blueberries
[Ingredients: Cream cheese filling]
- 8 oz (226 g) brick cream cheese, softened
- ⅓ cup (67 g) granulated sugar
- ½ tsp vanilla extract
[Ingredients: Streusel]
- ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- ¼ cup (50 g) packed brown sugar
- ⅛ tsp salt
- 2 tbsp butter, melted but not hot
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and line 15 muffin cups with paper liners.
- In a large bowl, whisk the oil and melted butter, then whisk in the sugar, egg and egg white. Whisk in the buttermilk and vanilla.
- In another bowl, whisk the flour, cornstarch, baking powder and salt. Fold into the wet ingredients until mostly combined, then fold in the blueberries just until combined.
- **Filling:** Stir the cream cheese, sugar and vanilla until smooth.
- **Streusel:** Whisk the flour, brown sugar and salt, then drizzle in the butter and toss with a fork until clumpy. Don't overmix.
- Spoon the batter into the cups about ⅔ full, saving a little. Make a hollow in each with a spoon, add about 2 tsp filling, cover with a spoonful of the reserved batter and swirl gently with a knife.
- Sprinkle the streusel over the tops and bake 25 minutes, until baked through.
- Cool about 10 minutes before serving. They sink a little as they cool, which is normal.
[Tips]
- Frozen blueberries can go in without thawing, though they may streak the batter purple.
- A standard tin holds 12, so bake the last 3 as a second batch, checking at about 17 minutes.
- Keeps 2 days airtight at room temperature, or 5 days in the fridge.

= Bread Pudding
makes: one 8-inch square pan
time: Prep 15 min · Bake 45 min
temp: 350°F / 175°C
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Ellenmarie | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 6 slices day-old bread, torn into small pieces
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
- ½ cup raisins
- 2 cups milk
- ¾ cup sugar
- 4 large eggs, beaten
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
- Spread the bread in an 8-inch square baking dish, drizzle with the butter and scatter the raisins over it.
- Whisk the milk, sugar, eggs, cinnamon and vanilla until well combined.
- Pour the custard over the bread and press down gently with a fork until it's all soaked.
- Bake about 45 minutes, until golden, set in the center and springy when pressed.
[Tips]
- Brioche or challah is especially good; French bread or any sturdy day-old loaf works too.
- Swap the raisins for dried cranberries, chopped apple, chocolate chips or nuts. A little nutmeg or a splash of bourbon adds warmth.
- Double it in a 9 × 13 dish for a crowd. It can be baked a day ahead and reheated.

= Country Pound Cake
makes: one 10-inch tube cake
time: Bake 1 hr 20 min, plus about 4 hr cooling
temp: 350°F / 175°C, starting in a cold oven
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Morgan | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- ¾ tsp baking powder
- ¼ tsp salt
- 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
- ½ cup shortening
- 2⅔ cups sugar
- 5 eggs
- 1 cup milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp lemon extract
[Method]
- Don't preheat the oven. Grease a 10-inch tube pan and line the bottom with parchment or wax paper.
- Sift together the flour, baking powder and salt.
- In a large bowl, beat the butter, shortening and sugar on low speed for 10 minutes. Beat in the eggs one at a time. Add the flour mixture and the milk alternately, mixing well, then the vanilla and lemon extracts.
- Pour the batter into the pan and put it in the cold oven. Set the oven to 350°F (175°C) and bake 1 hour 20 minutes, until a toothpick in the middle comes out clean.
- Cool in the pan 1 hour, then turn out onto a rack and cool at least 3 hours more.
[Tips]
- The cold-oven start is how the recipe was handed down to the author, who says it turns out perfectly every time.

= Pecan Pie Without Corn Syrup
makes: one 9-inch pie
time: Prep 15 min · Bake 40–50 min
temp: 400°F, then 350°F (200°C, then 175°C)
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Elaine Helms | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 2 large eggs, at room temperature
- 1 cup light brown sugar
- ½ cup unsalted butter, melted and cooled
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup chopped pecans, plus pecan halves to decorate (optional)
- 1 unbaked 9-inch pie shell
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
- Whisk the eggs in a large bowl until foamy, then whisk in the brown sugar, butter, sugar, flour, milk and vanilla until smooth.
- Fold in the chopped pecans and pour into the pie shell. Arrange pecan halves on top if you like.
- Bake 10 minutes, then turn the oven down to 350°F (175°C) and bake 30–40 minutes more, until set but still slightly wobbly in the middle.
- Cool completely before slicing; the filling firms up as it cools.
[Tips]
- Stir in ½ cup semisweet chocolate chips with the pecans, or add 1 tbsp bourbon with the vanilla.
- Bake the pie on a rimmed baking sheet to brown the crust evenly and catch drips.

= Pineapple Upside-Down Cake
serves: 9
time: Prep 15 min · Bake 50–55 min
temp: 350°F / 175°C
from: Betty Crocker | bettycrocker.com
[Ingredients: Topping]
- ¼ cup butter
- ⅔ cup packed brown sugar
- 9 pineapple slices in juice (from a 20-oz can), drained
- 9 maraschino cherries, stems removed (optional)
[Ingredients: Cake]
- 1⅓ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- ⅓ cup shortening
- 1½ tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt
- ¾ cup milk
- 1 egg
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Put the butter in a 9-inch square pan and let it melt in the oven as it heats. Sprinkle the brown sugar evenly over the butter, lay the pineapple slices on top and set a cherry in the center of each.
- Put all the cake ingredients in a medium bowl and beat with an electric mixer on low for 30 seconds, scraping the bowl constantly. Beat on high for 3 minutes, scraping occasionally. Pour the batter over the fruit.
- Bake 50–55 minutes, until a toothpick in the center comes out clean.
- Straight away, set a heatproof plate upside down over the pan and flip the two together (use hot pads). Leave the pan in place for a few minutes so the topping drips over the cake, then lift it off. Serve warm.
[Tips]
- Flip as soon as the topping stops bubbling, or the fruit can stick to the pan.
- Cranberries or pieces of crystallized ginger can stand in for the cherries.
