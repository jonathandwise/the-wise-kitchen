= Allrecipes Taco Seasoning
makes: about 1 ounce
time: 5 min
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Bill Echols | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1½ tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp paprika
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp onion powder
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- ¼ tsp dried oregano
[Method]
- Stir all the spices together in a small bowl.
- Store in an airtight container.
[Tips]
- Compared with the Homemade Taco Seasoning in this chapter, this one adds paprika, uses twice the salt and skips the cayenne.

= Best Steak Marinade in Existence
makes: about 1⅓ cups
time: 15 min
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Kookie | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- ½ cup olive oil
- ⅓ cup soy sauce
- ⅓ cup fresh lemon juice
- ¼ cup Worcestershire sauce
- 3 tbsp dried basil
- 1½ tbsp garlic powder
- 1½ tbsp dried parsley flakes
- 1 tsp ground white pepper
- 1 tsp dried minced garlic
- ¼ tsp hot sauce
[Method]
- Blend everything on high for about 30 seconds, until well combined.
- Pour into a zip-top bag with the steak, press out the air, seal and refrigerate up to 8 hours.
- Lift out the steak, shake off the excess and cook it however you like. Discard the used marinade.
[Tips]
- Unused marinade keeps up to 5 days in the fridge.

= Copycat Fast-Food Sauces
from: Cheap Meal Ideas (image shared on Reddit) | reddit.com
[Ingredients: Chick-fil-A–style sauce]
- ¼ cup mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp barbecue sauce
- 1 tbsp yellow mustard
- 2 tsp honey
- 1 tsp lemon juice
[Ingredients: Raising Cane's–style sauce]
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- ¼ cup ketchup
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp Worcestershire sauce
- ½ tsp black pepper
[Ingredients: Taco Bell–style Baja sauce]
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp minced fresh jalapeño
- 1 tbsp grated Parmesan
- 1 tsp white vinegar
- ¼ tsp ground cumin
[Ingredients: In-N-Out–style spread]
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- 3 tbsp ketchup
- 2 tbsp sweet pickle relish
- ½ tsp white vinegar
- ½ tsp sugar
[Ingredients: Wingstop-style ranch]
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- ½ cup buttermilk
- 1 packet (1 oz) dry ranch dressing mix
- Pinch of salt
[Ingredients: Panda Express–style orange sauce]
- ½ cup orange juice
- ¼ cup white vinegar
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- ¼ cup granulated sugar
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
[Method]
- **Creamy sauces:** Whisk each set of ingredients together in a small bowl until smooth. They taste best after a little time in the fridge for the flavors to come together.
- **Orange sauce:** Stir everything together in a small saucepan and simmer, stirring, until the sugar dissolves and the sauce reduces a little.
[Tips]
- The original card lists ingredients only; the method above is the usual way to put these together.
- For a glossy orange glaze that clings to chicken, stir in 1 tsp cornstarch mixed with 1 tbsp water while it simmers.

= Homemade Taco Seasoning
makes: about 2½ tbsp (enough for 1 lb of meat)
time: 5 min
from: Spend With Pennies | spendwithpennies.com
[Ingredients: Seasoning]
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1½ tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- Pinch of cayenne (optional)
[Ingredients: For taco meat]
- 1 lb lean ground beef or turkey
- 2 tbsp of the seasoning
- ½ cup water
- 1 tbsp tomato paste (optional)
[Method]
- Whisk all the spices together in a small bowl. Store airtight in a cool, dry place for up to 6 months.
- For taco meat, brown the beef or turkey in a large skillet over medium-high heat for 5–7 minutes, breaking it up, until no pink remains. Drain off the fat.
- Stir in 2 tbsp of the seasoning and cook for 1 minute.
- Add the water (and tomato paste, if using) and simmer uncovered until most of the liquid has cooked off. Serve in shells or tortillas.
[Tips]
- 2 to 2½ tbsp of this mix replaces one store-bought packet.
- Half a diced onion browned with the meat, and a diced tomato added with the tomato paste, are both good additions.

= Sweet and Sour Sauce
makes: 1 cup
time: Prep 7 min · Cook 5 min
from: Serious Eats · recipe by Joshua Bousel | seriouseats.com
note: A takeout-style dipping sauce in about 12 minutes. Pineapple juice and rice vinegar give it the classic sweet-tart balance.
[Ingredients]
- 1 tbsp cornstarch (7 g)
- 1 tbsp water (15 ml)
- ⅔ cup pineapple juice (160 ml)
- ⅓ cup rice vinegar (80 ml)
- ⅓ cup light brown sugar (74 g)
- 3 tbsp ketchup (45 ml)
- 1 tbsp soy sauce (15 ml)
[Method]
- Whisk the cornstarch and water together in a small bowl and set aside.
- In a medium saucepan, combine the pineapple juice, rice vinegar, brown sugar, ketchup and soy sauce and bring to a boil over medium heat.
- Stir in the cornstarch mixture and cook until thickened, 1–2 minutes. Take the pan off the heat.
[Tips]
- Use it right away, or keep it in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 2 weeks.
- Good with homemade fried chicken, egg rolls or anything else you'd dunk at a takeout counter.

= Szechuan Sauce
makes: a little over ½ cup
time: 10 min
from: Feasting at Home | feastingathome.com
[Ingredients]
- ½–1 tsp Szechuan peppercorns (or black peppercorns)
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- 3 tbsp honey (or maple syrup or brown sugar)
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar (or Chinese black vinegar)
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine or mirin (optional)
- 3 garlic cloves, finely minced
- 2 tsp finely minced fresh ginger
- 1 tbsp garlic chili paste or sambal oelek (or 1 tsp chili flakes)
- ½ tsp Chinese five-spice
- 2–3 tsp cornstarch (optional, to thicken)
[Method]
- Toast the peppercorns in a dry skillet over medium heat for 1–2 minutes, until fragrant, then crush them.
- Whisk everything except the cornstarch together in a bowl, or shake it in a jar.
- **As a stir-fry sauce:** Whisk in 2–3 tsp cornstarch. For more heat, toss 5–10 dried red chilies into the wok just before the sauce (they're for flavor, not for eating).
- **As a marinade:** Leave out the cornstarch and salt the chicken, tofu or beef, since the sauce alone isn't salty enough.
- **As a dipping sauce:** Whisk in 2–3 tsp cornstarch and simmer in a small saucepan for 1 minute.
[Tips]
- Keeps up to 7 days in the fridge.
- Szechuan peppercorns have a numbing tingle. Start with ½ tsp if you're unsure.
- Tamari is much saltier than soy sauce. If you use it, dilute 2½ tbsp tamari with 1½ tbsp water.

= Zaxby's Chicken Fingers Dipping Sauce
serves: 6
time: 10 min, plus 2 hr chilling
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Franny | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- ¼ cup ketchup
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp Worcestershire sauce
- ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper, or more to taste
[Method]
- Whisk the mayonnaise, ketchup and garlic powder together, then whisk in the Worcestershire until smooth.
- Sprinkle a layer of pepper over the surface and stir it in; repeat once more.
- Cover and refrigerate at least 2 hours so the flavors blend.
[Tips]
- It's the same mix as the Raising Cane's–style sauce in Copycat Fast-Food Sauces.
