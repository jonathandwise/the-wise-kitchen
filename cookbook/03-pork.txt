= Instant Pot Roast Pork with Brussels Sprouts and Sweet Potatoes
serves: 8
time: Prep 15 min · Cook 47 min
from: MyRecipes | myrecipes.com
note: A whole dinner from one pot: paprika-rubbed pork shoulder pressure-cooked on a bed of onions and tarragon, then sweet potatoes and Brussels sprouts cooked in the same broth.
[Ingredients]
- 2½ lb boneless pork shoulder (Boston butt), trimmed and cut into 4 pieces
- 1 tsp paprika
- ¾ tsp salt, divided
- ⅝ tsp freshly ground black pepper, divided
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 tbsp canola oil, divided
- 1 large onion, cut into 8 wedges
- 2 cups water
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh tarragon
- 1½ lb peeled sweet potatoes, cut into 2-inch pieces
- 1 lb Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
- ½ cup honey mustard
[Method]
- Pat the pork dry with paper towels. Mix the paprika, ½ tsp of the salt, ½ tsp of the pepper and the garlic, and rub it all over the pork.
- Set a 6-quart Instant Pot to Sauté (More). Add 2 tsp of the oil and brown the pork on all sides, about 8 minutes. Move it to a plate.
- Add the remaining 1 tsp oil and the onion wedges and cook, stirring constantly, until lightly browned, about 3 minutes. Stir in the water, scraping up the browned bits from the bottom. Set the pork on top of the onions, scatter over the tarragon and turn off Sauté.
- Lock the lid, set the valve to Sealing and cook at High Pressure for 32 minutes. Quick-release the pressure, lift out the pork and keep it warm.
- Add the sweet potatoes and Brussels sprouts to the onion broth in the pot. Lock the lid and cook at High Pressure for 4 minutes, then quick-release.
- Lift the vegetables onto a platter with a slotted spoon and sprinkle with the remaining ¼ tsp salt. Don't toss them, or the sweet potatoes will fall apart.
- Shred the pork into bite-size pieces and pile it next to the vegetables. Sprinkle with the remaining ⅛ tsp pepper and serve with the honey mustard.

= Italian Breaded Pork Chops
serves: 4
time: Prep 25 min · Cook 35 min
temp: 325°F / 160°C
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Elisaw | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 3 eggs, lightly beaten
- 3 tbsp milk
- 1½ cups Italian-seasoned breadcrumbs
- ½ cup grated Parmesan
- 2 tbsp dried parsley
- 4 pork chops
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, chopped
[Method]
- Heat the oven to 325°F (160°C).
- Beat the eggs and milk in a shallow dish. Mix the breadcrumbs, Parmesan and parsley in a second dish.
- Dip each chop in the egg, then coat it well in the crumbs.
- Heat the oil in a large ovenproof skillet over medium heat. Cook the garlic until just golden to flavor the oil, then lift it out (save it for another use).
- Fry the chops about 5 minutes per side, until golden and crisp.
- Move the skillet to the oven and bake 20–25 minutes, until the chops reach 145°F (63°C) in the center.
[Tips]
- No ovenproof skillet? Finish the chops on the stove over medium-low heat instead.
- For a thicker crust, dip the chops in egg and crumbs twice. A white wine and lemon butter sauce dresses them up.

= Pressure Cooker Pulled Pork
serves: 8
time: Prep 15 min · Cook 50 min
from: Home. Made. Interest. | homemadeinterest.com
[Ingredients]
- 2 lb pork shoulder
- 1 tbsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp dry mustard powder
- 2 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ cup BBQ sauce, plus more to taste
- ½ cup water
[Method]
- Cut the pork shoulder into two roughly 1-lb pieces.
- Mix the onion powder, garlic powder, mustard, salt and pepper and rub it all over the pork.
- Put the pork in the pressure cooker and pour in the BBQ sauce and water.
- Lock the lid, set the valve to Sealing and cook on High pressure for 40 minutes.
- Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then vent the rest.
- Lift the pork out (leave the liquid behind) and shred it with two forks. Toss with extra BBQ sauce to taste.

= Simple BBQ Ribs
serves: 4
time: Prep 5 min · Cook 1 hr 45 min
temp: 325°F / 165°C
from: Allrecipes · recipe by Lloyd Rushing | allrecipes.com
[Ingredients]
- 2½ lb country-style pork ribs
- 2 tbsp kosher salt
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 cup barbecue sauce
[Method]
- Put the ribs in a large pot, cover with water and stir in the salt, garlic powder and pepper. Bring to a boil over medium heat and boil 40–45 minutes, until tender.
- Meanwhile, heat the oven to 325°F (165°C).
- Move the ribs to a 9 × 13 baking dish, pour the barbecue sauce over them and cover with foil.
- Bake 1–1½ hours, until the pork reaches 160°F (71°C).

= Slow Cooker Carnitas
serves: 10–12
time: Prep 15 min · Slow cook 7–10 hr
from: RecipeTin Eats | recipetineats.com
[Ingredients]
- 4 lb (2 kg) boneless, skinless pork shoulder (or 5 lb / 2.5 kg bone-in)
- 2½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- ¾ cup orange juice (about 2 oranges)
- Oil, for crisping
[Ingredients: Rub]
- 1 tbsp dried oregano
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tbsp olive oil
[Method]
- Pat the pork dry and season it all over with the salt and pepper.
- Mix the oregano, cumin and olive oil and rub it over the pork.
- Set the pork in the slow cooker fat-side up. Scatter over the onion, jalapeño and garlic, then pour over the orange juice.
- Cook on Low for 10 hours or High for 7 hours, until it shreds easily.
- Lift out the pork, let it cool slightly and shred it with two forks. Skim the fat off the juices if you like. If there's more than 2 cups of juice, boil it down to about 2 cups and keep it (unstrained) for seasoning.
- **To crisp:** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large nonstick or well-seasoned skillet over high heat. Spread out a batch of pork, drizzle with some of the juice and leave it until the juice cooks off and the underside is golden and crusty. Flip and sear the other side briefly. Repeat in batches without crowding the pan.
- Drizzle with a little more juice just before serving.
[Tips]
- Leave some of the fat cap on (but remove any skin); you can skim the fat later.
- Smaller roast (2–3 lb boneless): 8 hours on Low.
- **Instant Pot:** 1 hr 30 min at High pressure with natural release, then shred and crisp as above.
- **Oven:** In a covered roasting pan with 2 cups water at 325°F (160°C) for 2 hours, then uncovered for 1–1½ hours more, adding water if needed. No need to pan-fry afterwards.
- Store the pork and juices separately: 3 days in the fridge, 3 months in the freezer.

= Slow Cooker Country-Style Ribs
serves: 4–6
time: Prep 15 min · Slow cook 4 hr
from: RecipeTeacher | recipeteacher.com
[Ingredients]
- 2–3 lb boneless country-style ribs
- 1 medium onion, sliced
- BBQ sauce, to serve (optional)
[Ingredients: Rub]
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp coarse salt
- 2 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp ground mustard
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp cayenne
[Ingredients: Cooking liquid]
- ¾ cup chicken broth
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp liquid smoke
[Method]
- Mix the rub ingredients and coat the ribs. If you have time, let them sit for up to an hour.
- Grease the slow cooker and scatter the onion over the bottom. Lay the ribs on top.
- Stir together the broth, vinegar, Worcestershire and liquid smoke and pour it over the ribs.
- Cook on Low for 4 hours.
- Lift the ribs out carefully, since they'll be very tender, and serve with the cooking juices or your favorite BBQ sauce.
